# Free resource licensing and sourcing

Kenney Food Kit, Furniture Kit, Retro Urban Kit and Interface Sounds were downloaded from the publisher's free download endpoints. Each installed subset carries the publisher's License.txt. CC0 permits commercial reuse, modification and redistribution without an attribution requirement. The asset license imposes no AI training or assisted-use restriction. Furniture and retro exterior supplements should be checked against the title's visual language before transfer. [Food Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit) · [Furniture Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit) · [Retro Urban Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/retro-urban-kit) · [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds)

Poly Haven Terrazzo Tiles is stored as hash-verified 1K base color, DirectX normal and roughness textures. CC0 grants commercial use, modification and redistribution without attribution and has no asset-level AI limitation. Preserve the downloaded source terms. Use roughness inverted in Unity's smoothness channel and import the normal as Normal Map. [Asset](https://polyhaven.com/a/terrazzo_tiles) · [License](https://polyhaven.com/license) · [Original download index](https://api.polyhaven.com/files/terrazzo_tiles)

Vintage Computer Keyboard was verified in Unity My Assets and acquired through the studio account. Preserve Asset Store license and raw-source restrictions. Game-level modification and incorporated commercial use are subject to the applicable Asset Store terms. Keep raw source private and verify provider and Asset Store rules before any AI training, model upload or other AI processing. [Unity Asset Store terms](https://unity.com/legal/as-terms)

Sonniss GameAudioGDC is deferred because the multigigabyte collections overlap the existing library. The publisher permits commercial production use without attribution. Raw source redistribution and AI/ML training are restricted. Confirm the current license before uploading any clips to an external AI service. [Publisher archive and license](https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc/)

Quaternius Furniture Pack and ambientCG Tiles074 remain alternatives. Use if the owned shelving, Kenney and Poly Haven subsets fail a specific test. The publisher sources advertise CC0. Keep claims tied to the exact source downloaded. [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/packs/furniture.html) · [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Tiles074)

The owned Unity KayKit Restaurant Bits edition was obtained, so there is one installation and no duplicate publisher download.
